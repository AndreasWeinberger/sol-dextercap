MANO PARAMETERS
- shape: 10 values defining how the hand looks anatomically (length of fingers, shape of palm, etc.)
- betas: 45 values defining rotations of each joint (x,y,z rotations for each of the 15 joints including root of the hand)

GETTING OUTPUT
- define hand_params as a dict (global orient, transl, hand_pose, betas)
- retrieve the output using >> smplx_output = smplx_model(**hand_parms)
- read the joints and vertices at index [0] respectively from the output tensor
